Why an overlay on iPhone

iPhone browsers can't make part of a page full screen. The overlay covers the whole window and the chat behind it is hidden from screen readers (inert).

The strip

  • Black, so it is clearly not part of the app (apps are framed white by default).
  • Exit always visible, with a word, 44 px or more, first in focus order.
  • Focus is kept inside: strip → app → strip.
  • Title is the app's name, written by the chat.
  • Menu: Download, Restart. Nothing else.
  • Top inset follows the safe area; the home indicator area stays clear.
