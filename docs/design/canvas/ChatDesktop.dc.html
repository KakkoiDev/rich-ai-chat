How many moons does Jupiter have, and which are the biggest?

Kakkoi Chat

Jupiter has 95 confirmed moons1. The four largest are the Galilean moons, found by Galileo in 16102:

  • Ganymede, the largest moon in the solar system, bigger than Mercury3
  • Callisto, heavily cratered
  • Io, the most volcanic body we know of
  • Europa, with an ice shell over a salty ocean4
