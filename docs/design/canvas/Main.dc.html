The Kakkoi style

カッコイイ・スタイル

Black text on white paper, set in Bebas Neue and Inter, divided by hairlines. Nothing else is added unless it carries meaning.

Twelve rules an agent can apply

  1. Two colours. Foreground #000 on background #fff (inverted in dark mode). Grey #595959 is for secondary text only.
  2. Colour means state. Red = error, green = success, amber = warning. Each always comes with a word or an icon. No accent colour, no brand colour.
  3. Hierarchy comes from type. Bebas Neue for the wordmark and section titles (capitals only, never sentences). Inter for everything people read. Noto Sans JP for Japanese, including Japanese headings.
  4. One column. Content sits in a centred column of 768 px at most. Panels (history, Activity, Tasks) sit beside it, never inside it.
  5. Hairlines, not boxes. Sections and list rows are separated by a 1 px rule. Use a full border only around things you can act on, or around third-party content.
  6. Square edges. Radius is 0 everywhere. No shadows, except a 4 px hard offset under floating menus so they read as on top.
  7. Underlined links, outlined buttons. One filled black button per area: the primary action. Every other action is outlined or plain text.
  8. 44 px targets, 8 px apart. Including chips, list rows and the Exit control.
  9. Focus is a 2 px outline with a 2 px gap, in the foreground colour. Hover fills grey or inverts; focus is never a colour change alone.
  10. Words over icons. Icons are Lucide outlines and appear next to a label. Icon-only buttons are allowed only for send, stop, close and menu, and always have an accessible name.
  11. The cube is the only motif. Use it for the app icon, the empty state and the loading mark. Never behind text people need to read. It stops moving when reduced motion is on.
  12. Calm motion. 120 ms for state changes, 200 ms for panels, nothing that bounces. Waiting is shown as words and elapsed time, not spinners alone.

Decisions (hand-back 6)

Brand hierarchyKAKKOI is the maker brand and owns the cube and the wordmark. KakkoiSchool is the programme the classrooms belong to; it is named in text, it has no mark of its own. The product is Kakkoi Chat (home-screen label "Kakkoi Chat", 11 characters). "Rich" describes engineering, not what people see.
Dark modeYes. Follows the system setting, with an override in Settings. Straight inversion, one table of values.
Generated appsThey get the inline subset: black and white, square, 44 px controls, system fonts. It reads as Kakkoi but adds no web fonts, so the 32 KB budget goes to the app. An app may override any of it.
Students and the modelStudents see which model answered and where it ran (privacy is their business too), but only a teacher can change it. The default is a local model, so nothing leaves the Mac unless a teacher chooses.
IconsLucide 1.59 (already vendored). New ones needed: maximize, minimize, download, smartphone-plus (install), list (Activity), list-checks (Tasks), rotate-ccw (retry), wifi-off (offline), share (iOS).
